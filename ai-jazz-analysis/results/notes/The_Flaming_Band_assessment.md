## Overall Impression

The chords fit the tonal/modal intricacies of jazz. Some repetition of chord progressions defines clear sections, but there are moments where it drifts into new/random territories. Clear sectioning of the different parts and timbre is pretty satisfactory. The swing feels consistent throughout the song, but over long periods there can be change. Overall, the micro-analysis is very good — shorter lengths of music sound fine. But in the context of the entire piece there are inconsistencies with style, swing ratio, chords, and sectioning: at 1:34 the piece moves from open modal jazz to a more classic swing feel.

### 1. Harmonic Authenticity — 3/5

Clear progression: Bb7→G7→Cm7→F7. No chord progression is felt, but the phantom chords created by the head (brass) do work with each other. It feels slightly random and no well-defined 2-5-1s can be found.

### 2. Swing Feel & Microtiming — 2/5

Swing is inconsistent — sometimes extremely good at being micro-coherent, sometimes lacking clear melody (piano sections much better than brass sections). The ending near 2:45 completely breaks down. As it's mostly alright during the head and solo, it gets a 2.

### 3. Improvisational Coherence — 3/5

The solo is micro-coherent but feels quite different from the rest of the song — a macro-coherence problem. The melody is aimless. However, the piano solo does sound good to a listener and has some merit: coherent motifs and runs are present.

### 4. Idiomatic Vocabulary — 2/5

The melody does not know how to use embellishments, motifs, and lines effectively. In the solo especially, the melody feels random and incoherent — not leading anywhere, just going in circles.

### 5. Ensemble Interaction — 4/5

Clear call-and-response feel throughout the piece. Each instrument does its job properly. The call and response is felt — probably the strongest part of this piece.

### 6. Formal Structure — 3/5

Well-defined A and B sections. Consistent, but doesn't follow known jazz formats properly (no well-defined chord pattern). It doesn't break its own rules, giving it a 3 in structure.

## Verdict

Even with a decent score, this piece feels too random and aimless for casual listeners to think it is human-made. A casual listener might sense something is off, and a practiced jazz musician can definitely tell. The starting brass melody does not feel correct — that is the most immediately detectable failure point.
