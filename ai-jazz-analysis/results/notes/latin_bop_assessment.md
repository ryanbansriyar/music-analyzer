## Overall Impression

It doesn't really encapsulate the true Latin feeling, but elements do exist. It is repetitive and generic but does properly follow jazz rules of melody, harmony, and interaction.

---

## Axis-by-Axis Scores

### 1. Harmonic Authenticity — 3/5

The chords make a decent level of sense, although it feels quite repetitive and sometimes chords don't really stand out or create harmonic progression.

---

### 2. Swing Feel & Microtiming — 3/5

The swing feels quite good for an untrained listener. However, as it tries to replicate Latin music, the clave is not properly developed and doesn't feel like a real clave. This makes it much worse in terms of Latin authenticity, and additionally the rhythm doesn't play around with the clave, leaving it quite basic.

---

### 3. Improvisational Coherence — 2/5

There is no solo in the song, however the melodic embellishments and variations of the original head exist and are okay.

---

### 4. Idiomatic Jazz Vocabulary — 3/5

The vocabulary is plain mediocre but makes sense, with chromatic and proper melodic movement. The melody is in style for the harmonic context.

---

### 5. Ensemble Interaction — 4/5

The interaction between instruments is good, with each part playing the proper role in Latin jazz. It just doesn't have the call-and-response effect that is required for proper interaction.

---

### 6. Formal Structure — 2/5

The structure isn't consistent to a high extent. There is some repetition of the melody, although random chords may be thrown in.

## Verdict

Passes as jazz, but fails as Latin jazz. The jazz harmony and ensemble interaction are present at a functional level, but the absence of a convincing clave, no proper solo, and structural inconsistency expose its limitations. A trained jazz musician would recognise it as generic and AI-like; a Latin music specialist would identify the rhythmic inauthenticity immediately.
